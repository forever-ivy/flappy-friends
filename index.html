Hyunjin × Felix — how to play

Hyunlix is a free fan-made flying game starring Hyunjin and Felix of Stray Kids. Pick a character, then tap the screen or press Space / ↑ to flap and slip through the gaps between emoji pipes to score. How far can you fly?

Three ways to fly

Choose Hyunjin, Felix, or Hyunlix duo mode flying with both together. Every run can save your score to the global leaderboard — sign up to keep your best runs and climb the ranking.

The 143 easter egg

Fans know why 143 matters. Reach a score of 143 in a single run and a special danmaku message rain celebrates your flight — a little love letter hidden in the game.

Play in your language

The game speaks English, Português, Español, and 한국어 — switch anytime from the language menu. No download, no install: it runs right in your browser, on desktop and mobile.
